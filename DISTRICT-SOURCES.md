# District reference set — 2 November 2026

This application offers 173 district reference entries to support filtering. This is a reconciled working directory, not a certified legal list. District status, spelling, activation and boundary definitions vary between dated sources. The original starter's Turbat label was replaced with Kech; Turbat is a city/tehsil alias rather than a separate district.

## Status notes

- Karezat appears on the Balochistan local-government page, while https://www.dawn.com/news/1979329 reports a dispute about restoring its district notification. It is retained as **status-under-review**, not counted as independently confirmed active. The dropdown includes it and shows the status note when selected.
- KP additions include Bar Swat (January 2026) and Paharpur (November 2025). ECP reference for Bar Swat: https://ecp.gov.pk/storage/uploads/zR2pNbmDLyqbGvZkiVwo1a7vEPSmlo4yHywsGMJ2.pdf . Older Census-2023 lists use older boundaries and lower totals. Central Dir/Allai and the South Waziristan split also need current notification reconciliation before legal/boundary uses.
- GB's reference list includes Darel, Tangir, Rondu and Gupis-Yasin: https://www.dawn.com/news/1488851/four-new-districts-set-up-in-gb . Notification and administrative activation dates differ; do not use this dataset to assert current operational status of district offices.
- No official district websites were generated from guessed domain names. Only recorded destinations appear; local-only results can be empty.
- Utility coverage is at district-directory granularity, not exact licence polygons. NEPRA reference: https://nepra.org.pk/licensing/Distribution%20XWDISCOs.php . A district may contain boundaries/exceptions not modelled here.
