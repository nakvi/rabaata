# Dataset provenance

Imported on 2026-11-02 from https://pakistangov.online/directory . The source page listed 194 federal, provincial and regulatory destinations. All 194 entries are accounted for in src/data/import-manifest.json. This project retains ten additional resources from the first release for 204 total listings.

Only factual listing names and destination URLs are used. The source site's explanatory copy, styling, page counts and answer content are not reproduced. New generic descriptions are generated from listing names, not copied guides. Discovery provenance is recorded separately from the agency's sourceUrl.

Import is not verification. Geographic assignment and category assignment are editorial classifications. Source directories can themselves be incomplete or contain stale URLs. Review each department destination before claiming current availability, service coverage, fees, eligibility or official endorsement. The live source may change after this snapshot.

Favicons are requested from https://www.google.com/s2/favicons at runtime with a category-based fallback. These are website icons and may be generic, stale or unavailable; they are not evidence of official affiliation. To avoid third-party icon requests, place approved icons in public/icons and set each record's iconUrl to its local path.
