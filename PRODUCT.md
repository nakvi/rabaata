# Product context

Raabta helps people find official Pakistani government websites and apps by location and task. It is independent and must never imply government endorsement. Users include citizens unfamiliar with department names, students, businesses and overseas Pakistanis.

Constraints: Next.js/React, static JSON, static export to Vercel, no hosted database, no Laravel, no fake accounts or submissions. Broader service coverage must survive district filtering. App downloads should use verified official agency/store destinations. Do not invent requirements, fees, processing times or verification badges.

Primary task: select region/district, search or filter resources, understand coverage and source status, then open the official destination. Secondary task: browser-local bookmarks.
